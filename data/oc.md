## Newport Beach Central Library (newportbeachlibrary.org)
- Address: 1000 Avocado Avenue, Newport Beach, CA 92660. Phone: (949) 717-3800.
- Hours: Monday to Thursday 9am to 9pm; Friday and Saturday 9am to 6pm; Sunday 12 noon to 5pm.
- Study rooms are available at the Central Library (only branch with them).
- Parking: the library lot, or the Civic Center parking structure at 100 Civic Center Dr, entered from Civic Center Drive at Avocado Avenue.

## UC Irvine dining halls (UCI Dining, uci.mydininghub.com)
- Two all-you-care-to-eat dining halls: The Anteatery in Mesa Court housing (4001 Mesa Rd, Irvine) and Brandywine in Middle Earth housing (557 E. Peltason Dr, Irvine).
- Lunch on campus: The Anteatery (Mesa Court) and Brandywine (Middle Earth) both serve lunch 11:30am to 2:00pm. Dinner at both: 5:00pm to 7:00pm. Times can change; Brandywine can close without notice.
- The Anteatery has 8 food stations; Brandywine has 9, named after meals in The Lord of the Rings.

## Crystal Cove State Park (parks.ca.gov/CrystalCove)
- Address: 8471 North Coast Highway, Laguna Beach, CA 92651, on Pacific Coast Highway between Corona del Mar and Laguna Beach. Park phone: (949) 494-3539.
- Day-use areas open 6am to sunset. The Historic District is open 6am to 10pm.
- Parking per vehicle: Los Trancos lot $5 per hour; other lots $15 per day, or $20 per day on holidays and summer weekends.
- From the Los Trancos lot (inland side of Pacific Coast Highway), walk the half-mile nature trail through the tunnel under the highway to the Historic District, or take the shuttle. The visitor center cottage explains the 36 historic cottages (1935 to 1955).
- Food: the Beachcomber Cafe (in the Historic District, steps from the sand) and the Crystal Cove Shake Shack.
- 3.2 miles of beach in seven coves. Offshore is a marine conservation area.
- Tide pools are best at low tide. Look but don't touch: collecting shells, rocks or anything natural is prohibited by law.
- No dogs on the beach or in the backcountry (service animals excepted); on paved areas dogs must be on a leash of 6 feet or less.
- Moro Canyon hike: park in the Moro Canyon day-use area, about a mile in, mostly flat; turn back at the junction with East Cut Across Trail.
- Safety: high surf and dangerous rip currents; ask lifeguard staff about the day's conditions. Stay on trails and away from the cliff faces, which are unstable. No fires on the sand. No alcohol except at concessions and Moro Campground.

## Huntington Beach (huntingtonbeachca.gov)
- Lifeguard Headquarters: 103 Pacific Coast Highway, Huntington Beach. Phone (714) 536-5281 (office Monday to Friday 8am to 5pm). Emergency: call 911.
- The beach closes at 10pm; the pier closes at midnight.
- 127 fire pits, from south of First Street to Beach Blvd. Barbecues must be within 6 feet of a fire pit and up off the sand.
- No smoking, alcohol or drugs on the beach or pier. No bikes or skateboards on the pier. No surfing where blackball flags are up.
- Dogs only on the service road and at Dog Beach, on leash.
- Swim near a lifeguard, never swim alone, and don't fight the current.
- Caught in a rip current (pulled out by the water): don't fight it. Swim parallel to shore until you're out of the pull, then swim back to shore at an angle. If you can't, float, wave and yell for a lifeguard.

## Safety
- Emergency: call 911. Huntington Beach Lifeguard Headquarters: (714) 536-5281.
- Caught in a rip current (pulled out by the water): don't fight it. Swim parallel to shore until you're out of the pull, then swim back to shore at an angle. If you can't, float, wave and yell for a lifeguard.
- Crystal Cove: stay on trails and away from the cliff faces, which are unstable. No fires on the sand.
